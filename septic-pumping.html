Septic tank pumping without the runaround.

Routine pumping is one of the simplest ways to protect a septic system. Bulldog Septic provides pump-outs from its Terrebonne base for Central Oregon properties.

Routine maintenance

Do not wait for a backup to think about the tank.

Solids build up in every septic tank. Pumping removes that material before it can contribute to clogs, overflow or problems farther down the system.

FAQ

Septic pumping questions.

How often should a septic tank be pumped?

There is no single schedule for every home. Tank size, household occupancy, water use and solids accumulation all matter. If you do not know your tank's recent history, call to discuss your system.

Should I pump the tank if everything seems fine?

Yes, pumping is preventive maintenance. Waiting until sewage backs up can turn a routine service into a much larger problem.

What if I cannot find the septic lid?

Let Bulldog Septic know when scheduling. Tank access and site conditions can be discussed before service.
